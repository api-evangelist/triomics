# Triomics

> Triomics is an AI healthcare company focused on oncology workflows. Its AI agents transform unstructured electronic health records (HL7, FHIR, CCDA, PDF, and imaging formats) into structured, cited, auditable datasets for cancer centers and life sciences organizations. Products include PRISM (AI-powered patient-to-trial matching), Symphony (visit preparation), Harmony (registry data curation and abstraction for NAACCR, SEER, COC, and QOPI measures), and OncoLLM, a language model that reads entire patient charts to answer oncology-specific questions. The platform integrates with Epic, OncoEMR, iKnowMed, and Cerner, and is approved on the Epic App Marketplace.

Triomics does not currently publish a public developer portal, API documentation, OpenAPI specification, or client SDKs. Integration is delivered inside EMRs (Epic App Marketplace, OncoEMR, iKnowMed, Cerner) rather than through a public API surface.
